# NOP prediction objective readiness

> This readiness assessment predates the exploratory work. A confirmed-date invoice subset was later prepared and two small-sample models evaluated; the approved business objective remains blocked. See [the final completion report](nop_actual_data_ml_exploratory_completion_report.md).

**Assessment date:** 3 November 2026  
**Decision:** No actual-data objective is ready for exploratory ML training from the evidence currently available. Several objectives are conditionally feasible for owner discussion, but none has a sufficiently defensible target/history for chronological model evaluation. No target series was constructed and no original source was transformed.

## Evidence reviewed

The existing readiness assessment recursively discovered 17 original files (14 Excel workbooks, 3 CSV files), 20 tables and 521 columns. It reopened each source through the project adapters, rechecked table counts and headers, and matched all 17 source hashes to the recorded manifest. Current verification also found **17/17 source hashes unchanged**. The reports preserve full-table quality findings, source authority/overlap evidence, date ambiguities, identifier and relationship analyses, target candidates and preparation constraints.

The existing readiness report is dated 3 November 2026 and retains a 2 November diagnostic date cutoff. Its source profiles distinguish native, strictly resolved, ambiguous and inferred dates. No date format, missing value, zero, target, relationship or business meaning is inferred in this review.

## Authorization and preparation gates

The decision response log contains 17 decisions and zero confirmed approvals. D01–D13 are core to the invoice-volume candidate; D12 is Proposed and the rest are Unresolved. The readiness workbook independently records zero approval flags. No exact NOP `SemanticBinding`, current approval, target/observation specification, governed readiness result, preparation plan approval or execution authorization was supplied. No authenticated NOP runtime or external approval store was connected.

Exploratory status can label an assumption; it cannot make an ambiguous date, incomplete period, unverified target, invalid join or insufficient holdout into defensible production evidence. The worker now has a generic observed-period grouping and distinct-count operation; the exploratory run exercised its trusted operator implementation without creating semantic approvals or a controlled worker receipt. The business execution and artifact publication lifecycle still require their existing approvals.

## Objective/model suitability

No objective above currently has a training dataset that supports a meaningful chronological comparison. Therefore the feasible-objective model comparison set is empty: **no MAE, RMSE, R², MAPE/SMAPE, baseline result, champion, or actual-data model artifact exists for this task.** In particular, 10 partial observed months do not support SARIMA/SARIMAX or Prophet seasonality evaluation; a tabular regressor on a handful of aggregated months would not create an honest validation set.

For future approved data, tabular regression families may be compared for an approved value/quantity outcome with adequate independent observations; time-series families require a regular mature series and chronological holdout. Classification requires a defensible eligible cohort and outcome window. These are suitability conditions, not algorithm-to-question mappings.

## Exact next evidence needed

1. NOP process/business owners answer D01–D13 in `reports/analysis/initial_prediction_business_clarification.md`, selecting the decision, target, population, scope, bucket, horizon, cadence and evaluation/acceptance criteria.
2. ERP/source and data-operations owners provide an authoritative production source/version, source owner, watermark/closure/completeness evidence and sufficient mature history for training plus protected chronological validation.
3. ERP/document owners verify invoice identity scope and source-specific date/event interpretation using source evidence; process/finance owners define invoice lifecycle, cancellation, return and adjustment rules. Preserve unresolvable rows as excluded/unknown with measured impact.
4. For revenue/profit, supply accounting-recognized revenue, actual cost/expense and adjustment/settlement data on an aligned entity, period and currency basis. For product/inventory/propensity, supply the missing approved item/customer identities, units, repeated snapshots/cohorts and outcomes listed in the table.
5. Record authenticated, scoped semantic, target, preparation, execution and (if business use is requested) publication approvals. Then build only the approved dataset and choose models according to its actual history and task structure.

Until that evidence exists, retain original source bytes and report candidate readiness rather than manufacturing observations or predictions.
